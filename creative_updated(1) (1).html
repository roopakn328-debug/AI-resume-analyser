Gmail Notification Setup

Scanline can email a candidate the moment you finish reviewing their resume, sent through your real Gmail account using EmailJS (a free browser-to-email service — no backend needed). Create a free account at emailjs.com, add Gmail as an Email Service, make a template with the variables {{to_email}}, {{to_name}}, {{subject}} and {{message}}, then paste the three IDs it gives you below.

Haven't set this up yet? No problem — every candidate's profile still has a "Notify via Gmail" button that opens a pre-filled Gmail compose window addressed to that candidate, so you can send the notification yourself in one click, using your own Gmail account, with nothing to configure.
